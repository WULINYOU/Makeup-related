## 16-Hour Wear Testing Protocol

Every setting product review follows our comprehensive wear test:

1. **Application Documentation:** Step-by-step photos during application.
2. **Immediate Assessment:** Full-face photos in natural, office, and flash lighting.
3. **4-Hour Check:** Blotting test, transfer assessment, color shift documentation.
4. **8-Hour Check:** After meal wear test, crease photography, fade evaluation.
5. **12-Hour Check:** End-of-workday assessment with comparison photos.
6. **16-Hour Final:** End-of-day assessment with full comparison documentation.
7. **Skin Type Analysis:** Results broken down by oily, dry, combination, and sensitive skin.
8. **Climate Testing:** Humidity chamber testing (30%, 60%, 80% humidity).
