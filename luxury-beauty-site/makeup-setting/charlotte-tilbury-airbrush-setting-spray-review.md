## How to Use Airbrush Flawless Setting Spray

### Method 1: As a Setting Spray (Most Common)
1. Complete your full makeup routine
2. Hold bottle 6-8 inches from face
3. Mist in an "X" then "T" pattern (3-4 sprays total)
4. Let dry naturally (about 30 seconds)
5. **Result:** Makeup locked in for up to 16 hours

### Method 2: As a Primer
1. Apply moisturizer and SPF
2. Mist setting spray onto clean skin
3. Let dry completely
4. Apply foundation and the rest of your makeup
5. **Result:** Smoother application, longer wear

### Method 3: As a Touch-Up Refresher
1. Throughout the day, mist over makeup as needed
2. Gently press with a tissue if there's excess oil
3. **Result:** Makeup refreshed and revived

### Pro Tips
- **Don't over-saturate** — 3-4 sprays is enough for the whole face
- **Eyes closed** — mist with eyes closed to avoid irritation
- **Layer with powder** — for oily skin, set with powder first, then spray
- **Travel size for touch-ups** — keep the 1.1 oz in your bag
