# About Luxury Beauty Edit

Welcome to Luxury Beauty Edit, your trusted source for independent, expert-curated reviews of the world's finest beauty products.

## Our Mission

We believe that luxury beauty products should deliver on their promises. Our mission is to provide honest, thorough, and scientifically-backed reviews that help you make informed decisions about high-end skincare, fragrances, and makeup.

Every review we publish is based on extensive testing, ingredient analysis, and real-world usage. We purchase all products at retail price—no free samples, no sponsored reviews, no exceptions.

## What We Review

### Luxury Skincare
Deep-dive reviews of prestigious brands including Augustinus Bader, La Prairie, La Mer, Sisley, and Clé de Peau. We analyze ingredient formulations, test efficacy over 8-12 weeks, and document real results across diverse skin types.

### Tinted & Sun Protection
Expert guides to luxury sunscreens, tinted moisturizers, and glow products from brands like Drunk Elephant, Supergoop, and Tatcha. We test wearability across skin tones and evaluate ingredient safety.

### Makeup & Setting
Comprehensive reviews of luxury makeup and setting products from Charlotte Tilbury, Pat McGrath, Tom Ford, and more. We conduct 16-hour wear tests and photograph results in multiple lighting conditions.

### Niche Fragrances
In-depth analysis of distinctive fragrances from Le Labo, Byredo, Maison Francis Kurkdjian, MUGLER, and Maison Margiela. We test longevity, projection, and seasonal suitability.

## Our Testing Methodology

Every product review follows our rigorous 8-step process:

1. **Purchase**: We buy all products at retail price. No PR samples accepted.
2. **Ingredient Analysis**: Cosmetic chemists review the full INCI list for efficacy and safety.
3. **Patch Testing**: 48-hour patch test on 30+ testers with diverse skin types and tones.
4. **Blind Testing**: 2-week blind test against 3 competitors in the same price range.
5. **Instrumental Measurement**: Corneometer for hydration, VISIA for texture improvement, and other professional tools.
6. **Long-Term Use**: Minimum 8 weeks of daily use by our core testing panel.
7. **Value Analysis**: Cost-per-use calculation and comparison to similar products.
8. **Final Scoring**: Standardized 1-10 scale across efficacy, texture, scent, and value.

## Editorial Standards

### Independence
We maintain strict editorial independence. Brands cannot pay for favorable coverage or influence our ratings. Our revenue comes from affiliate commissions, which do not affect our reviews.

### Transparency
We clearly disclose affiliate relationships and explain our testing methodology. If a product doesn't meet our standards, we say so—regardless of price or brand prestige.

### Expertise
Our editorial team includes beauty editors, cosmetic chemists, dermatologists, and fragrance specialists with decades of combined experience in the luxury beauty industry.

### Diversity
We test products on diverse skin types (dry, oily, combination, sensitive) and Fitzpatrick skin tones I-VI to ensure our recommendations work across different needs.
