## Our Testing Methodology

Our tinted product reviews go beyond marketing claims:

1. **Skin Tone Diversity:** Tested on Fitzpatrick types I-VI for color matching.
2. **Wear Testing:** 8-hour wear evaluation with oxidation and fade assessment.
3. **Ingredient Analysis:** Full INCI breakdown for comedogenic and irritant potential.
4. **Photography:** Standardized photos in natural, office, and flash lighting.
5. **Mixability Test:** Evaluated when mixed with moisturizers, SPF, and foundations.
6. **Climate Testing:** Performance in humidity (30%, 60%, 80%).
7. **Real-World Scenarios:** Office, outdoor, gym, and event testing.
