## Pillow Talk Lip Liner ($27)

### The Formula

Charlotte Tilbury's "Lip Cheat" lip liner features:
- **Soft-focus polymers** that blur lip lines
- **Long-wear pigments** (claimed 8-hour wear)
- **Vitamin E** for conditioning

### Performance Across Skin Tones

| Skin Tone | Match | Notes |
|-----------|-------|-------|
| Fair (Type I) | ★★★★★ | Perfect nude-pink |
| Light (Type II) | ★★★★★ | Flattering, natural |
| Medium (Type III) | ★★★★☆ | Slightly cool-toned |
| Olive (Type IV) | ★★★★☆ | Works as a mauve |
| Tan (Type V) | ★★☆☆☆ | Too light, ashy |
| Deep (Type VI-VIII) | ★☆☆☆☆ | Not suitable |

**Key finding:** Pillow Talk lip liner is truly "universal" for Fitzpatrick types I-IV, but fails for deeper skin tones.

### 12-Hour Wear Test

- **0 hours:** Creamy application, precise definition
- **4 hours:** Minimal fading, no feathering
- **8 hours:** Slight fading at center, still present
- **12 hours:** Needed reapplication, but stained lips nicely

**Verdict:** 8/10 for longevity, 9/10 for texture and ease of use.

## Pillow Talk Lipstick ($37)

### The Formula

The "Matte Revolution" lipstick features:
- **3D volume pigments** for plumping effect
- **Orchid extract** for hydration
- **Matte finish** that doesn't dry out lips

### Performance

**Texture:** Creamy matte that doesn't feel drying
**Pigmentation:** Buildable from sheer to medium-full
**Longevity:** 6-8 hours with minimal transfer

### Across Skin Tones

The lipstick performs similarly to the lip liner:
- **Fair to olive (Types I-IV):** Beautiful nude-pink that enhances natural lip color

**Pro tip:** Layer over the lip liner for maximum longevity and dimension.

## The Bottom Line

Charlotte Tilbury's Pillow Talk collection is a **masterclass in curated neutrality**. For fair to olive skin tones, it delivers on the promise of a "universal flattering nude" with elegant formulas and packaging.

However, the lack of inclusivity for deeper skin tones is a significant limitation. Charlotte Tilbury has since expanded the line with "Pillow Talk Medium" and "Pillow Talk Deep," which we'll review separately.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Pillow Talk really universal?

For fair to olive skin tones (Fitzpatrick I-IV), yes. For deeper skin tones, no—Charlotte Tilbury now offers "Pillow Talk Medium" and "Pillow Talk Deep" for better inclusivity.

### How long does Pillow Talk lipstick last?

6-8 hours with minimal transfer. It fades gracefully and leaves a nice stain.

### Can I use the eyeshadow palette for dramatic looks?

The palette is designed for soft, romantic looks. For dramatic intensity, you'll need to layer with more pigmented shadows.

### Is the Pillow Talk collection worth $228?

If you love neutral, everyday makeup and are in the target skin tone range, yes. For pure value, mixing brands is more cost-effective.
