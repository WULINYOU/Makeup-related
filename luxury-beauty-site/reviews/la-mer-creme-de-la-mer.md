## Ingredient Analysis

### The Star: Miracle Broth™

La Mer's proprietary **Miracle Broth™** is a fermented blend of sea kelp, vitamins, and minerals. While La Mer claims it "promotes cell turnover and hydration," our cosmetic chemistry analysis reveals:

**What it actually does:**
- **Sea kelp extract** provides amino acids and minerals that support the skin barrier
- **Fermentation** increases bioavailability of antioxidants
- **Clinical data** (published in *Journal of Cosmetic Dermatology*, 2019) shows 23% improvement in hydration after 8 weeks

**What it doesn't do:**
- It's not a "miracle" ingredient—results are consistent with high-quality marine extracts
- The concentration is undisclosed, making it impossible to verify potency

### Full INCI Breakdown

**Key Actives:**
- **Miracle Broth™** (sea kelp, vitamins, minerals): Hydration and barrier support
- **Laminaria Saccharina** (brown algae): Antioxidant and moisturizing
- **Magnesium Sulfate**: Texture enhancer (not a concern at this concentration)
- **Petrolatum**: Occlusive that locks in moisture (effective but not "luxury")
- **Mineral Oil**: Emollient (controversial but safe and effective)
- **Fragrance**: Added scent (potential irritant for sensitive skin)

**Preservatives:**
- **Phenoxyethanol**: Standard preservative, safe at <1%
- **Potassium Sorbate**: Food-grade preservative

**What's missing:**
- No retinoids, peptides, or vitamin C (common in anti-aging products)
- No niacinamide or hyaluronic acid (though the algae provides similar benefits)

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is La Mer Crème de la Mer worth the price?

For dry/mature skin that responds well to rich moisturizers, yes. The 90-day test showed measurable hydration and texture improvement. However, if you're on a budget, Tatcha Dewy Skin offers comparable results at lower cost.

### How long does 1 oz of La Mer last?

With twice-daily use, 1 oz lasts approximately 6 weeks. A little goes a long way—use a pea-sized amount for the entire face.

### Can I use La Mer if I have sensitive skin?

Most testers with sensitive skin tolerated it well, but it does contain fragrance. If you're fragrance-sensitive, patch test first or consider the fragrance-free version.

### What's the difference between La Mer Crème de la Mer and The Concentrate?

Crème de la Mer is a moisturizer focused on hydration and barrier support. The Concentrate is a treatment serum with higher concentrations of actives for targeted concerns like scars and discoloration.
