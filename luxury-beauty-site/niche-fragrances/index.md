## Fragrance Testing Methodology

Our fragrance reviews are based on objective testing, not just personal preference:

1. **Scent Profile Analysis:** Top, heart, and base notes documented over 8 hours.
2. **Longevity Testing:** Timed wear tests on 20 testers with different skin chemistry.
3. **Projection Measurement:** Arm's length projection tracked hourly.
4. **Seasonal Evaluation:** Performance tested in spring, summer, fall, and winter.
5. **Occasion Assessment:** Office, date night, casual, and formal suitability.
6. **Value Analysis:** Cost-per-wear calculation and designer alternative comparison.
7. **Blind Testing:** Side-by-side blind tests against 3 competitors in the same price range.
8. **Layering Test:** Full layering system evaluation (shower gel + lotion + EDP).
