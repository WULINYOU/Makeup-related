On A Date is a **floral-fruity fragrance** with a sophisticated structure that evolves beautifully over time:

## How to Wear On A Date

### Application Tips
1. **Moisturize first** — apply unscented lotion to pulse points to extend longevity
2. **Spray on pulse points** — wrists, neck, behind ears, inside elbows
3. **Don't rub** — let the fragrance dry naturally to preserve the scent structure
4. **Hair mist** — spray a cloud and walk through it for subtle, lingering scent
5. **Layer with travel spray** — carry the 0.3 oz for touch-ups

### Longevity Tips
EDT concentration means 4-6 hours of wear. To extend:
- **Layer with unscented products** — moisturizer creates a better base
- **Spray on clothing** — fabric holds scent longer than skin
- **Reapply strategically** — use travel spray mid-day
- **Store properly** — keep away from heat and light to preserve the formula

### For Couples
On A Date is designed to be shared. Try:
- **Both wearing it** — creates a beautiful shared scent memory
- **Layering with other Replica scents** — pair with Jazz Club (him) or By the Fireplace (her) for a complementary experience
