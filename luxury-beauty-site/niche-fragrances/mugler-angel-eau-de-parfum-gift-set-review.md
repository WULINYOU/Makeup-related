## The Scent Profile: What Does Angel Smell Like?

Angel is not a subtle fragrance. It's bold, sweet, and unapologetically rich. Here's the breakdown:

### Top Notes (First 15 Minutes)
- **Bergamot** — bright citrus opening that prevents the scent from being too heavy
- **Coconut** — tropical sweetness that's creamy but not cloying
- **Melon** — fresh fruitiness that adds complexity

### Heart Notes (15 Minutes to 2 Hours)
- **Praline** — the signature gourmand note; warm, nutty, caramel-like sweetness
- **Chocolate** — rich and indulgent without being food-like
- **Honey** — adds depth and natural sweetness

### Base Notes (2 Hours to 10+ Hours)
- **Patchouli** — earthy, woody, and incredibly long-lasting
- **Vanilla** — warm, comforting, and universally loved
- **Amber** — resinous warmth that anchors the sweetness

**The Overall Effect:** Imagine standing in a Parisian pâtisserie surrounded by warm caramel, chocolate, and vanilla, with a hint of earthy patchouli grounding the sweetness. That's Angel.

## How to Wear Angel

### Application Tips
1. **Start with shower gel** — creates a scented base
2. **Apply body lotion** — focus on pulse points (wrists, neck, décolletage)
3. **Spray EDP** — hold 6 inches from skin; spray wrists, neck, and behind ears
4. **Don't rub** — let the fragrance dry naturally to preserve the scent structure
5. **Touch up with travel spray** — carry for mid-day refresh

### The "Cloud" Technique
MUGLER recommends spraying Angel in the air and walking through the mist. This distributes the fragrance evenly across skin and hair for a more subtle effect.

### Layering for Longevity
- **Morning:** Shower gel + lotion + EDP
- **Midday:** Travel spray touch-up
- **Evening:** Reapply EDP for events

Result: **12-24 hours of fragrance wear**

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long does Angel last?

With proper layering (shower gel + lotion + EDP), Angel lasts 12-24 hours. The EDP alone lasts 8-10 hours.

### Is Angel too strong for everyday wear?

Angel has strong projection. For daytime or office wear, apply sparingly (1-2 sprays). For evening events, you can be more generous.

### Can I wear Angel in summer?

Angel is best suited for fall/winter. For summer, consider Angel Aqua or Angel Muse, which are lighter interpretations.

### Is the gift set worth it?

Absolutely. You save 40-50% compared to buying items separately, and the layering system makes the fragrance last much longer.

### Is Angel unisex?

While marketed as a women's fragrance, Angel's patchouli-heavy base makes it popular with men as well. Many consider it truly unisex.

### Does it stain clothes?

Angel contains colorants (CI 60730, CI 42090). Avoid spraying directly on light-colored fabrics. Spray on skin or dark clothing instead.
