## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I use this every night?

Yes, for dry and normal skin types. Combination and oily skin should start with 2-3 times per week.

### Does it replace my night cream?

It can. It's designed as a 2-in-1 overnight mask and moisturizer. If your skin is very dry, you can layer it over a serum or use your regular moisturizer on top.

### Is it safe for sensitive skin?

Most sensitive skin users tolerate it well, but it does contain natural fragrance components. Patch test first.
