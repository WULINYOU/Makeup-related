## How to Use Augustinus Bader The Rich Cream

### Morning Routine
1. Cleanse with a gentle, pH-balanced cleanser
2. Apply toner or essence (optional)
3. Dispense a pea-sized amount of The Rich Cream
4. Warm between fingertips and press into skin
5. Follow with SPF 30+ sunscreen

### Evening Routine
1. Double cleanse to remove makeup and sunscreen
2. Apply any treatment serums (vitamin C, retinol—use on alternate nights)
3. Apply The Rich Cream as the final step
4. For extra dry areas, layer a drop of facial oil on top

### Usage Frequency
- **Start:** Once daily (evening) for the first week
- **Build up:** Twice daily (morning and evening) as skin adjusts
- **Maintenance:** Continue twice daily for optimal results
