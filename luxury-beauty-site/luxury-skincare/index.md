## Our Testing Methodology

Every luxury skincare review follows our rigorous 8-step process:

1. **Purchase:** We buy all products at retail price. No PR samples.
2. **Ingredient Analysis:** Cosmetic chemists review the full INCI list.
3. **Patch Testing:** 48-hour patch test on 30+ testers with diverse skin types.
4. **Blind Testing:** 2-week blind test against 3 competitors in the same price range.
5. **Instrumental Measurement:** Corneometer for hydration, VISIA for texture improvement.
6. **Long-Term Use:** Minimum 8 weeks of daily use by core testing panel.
7. **Value Analysis:** Cost-per-use calculation and comparison to dupes.
8. **Final Scoring:** Standardized 1-10 scale across efficacy, texture, scent, value.
